## Sentence Breakdown

- One of the clearest signs of emotional immaturity is punishing someone
- rather than communicating with them.
- Withholding affection, giving the silent treatment,
- making them guess what they did wrong,
- creating distance
- just to make them feel the consequences of upsetting you.
- And to be clear, boundaries are not punishment.
- Taking space to regulate is not punishment.
- But using your hurt to make someone else suffer is.
- Emotionally mature. People can say, hey,
- this hurt me. I need to take a minute,
- and I want to talk about this when I'm calmer.
- At the end of the day, the goal is repair.
- The goal is deeper understanding and closer connection.
- And part of emotional maturity is learning how to be hurt
- without becoming hurtful. Which,
- by the way, just takes practice.
- Bye.

## Summary

这段视频区分了情绪不成熟的惩罚行为与健康的界限。故意收回关爱、冷战、让对方猜自己做错了什么，或者刻意疏远以让对方付出代价，都属于用伤痛去惩罚别人。相反，设立界限或暂时拉开距离来调节情绪，并不是惩罚。情绪成熟的人能够直接说明自己受到了伤害，需要暂停一下，并等平静后再谈。最终目标是修复关系、加深理解和亲密感；真正的成熟，是学会在受伤时不去伤人。

## Review Notes

- `rather than` 表示“而不是”，在本段中将“惩罚”与“沟通”形成鲜明对比。
- `give someone the silent treatment` 是常见搭配，指故意拒绝与某人说话。
- `take space to regulate` 强调暂停是为了调节自己，而不是让对方难受，这是它与惩罚的关键区别。
- `be hurt without becoming hurtful` 利用 `hurt` 和 `hurtful` 的词形关联形成有力的对照，可作为整段的核心复述句。
